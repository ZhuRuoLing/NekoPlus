# Laser Etching Recipe

<structure id="./structures/laser_etching_recipe.snbt"/>

The laser beam shown in the structure provides 64 laser levels.

Laser etching recipes can be used to produce materials required to craft items such as the <ref item="nekoplus:integrated_chip_circuit_board"/>.

The stamping platform used by a laser etching recipe must be irradiated by a laser beam with at least 64 laser levels, but the beam does not have to come from a High Energy Laser.

Everything else is the same as basic item processing/stamping.
