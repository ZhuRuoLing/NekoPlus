# Particle Stabilizer

<item id="nekoplus:particle_stabilizer"/>

Used to stabilize particles, or to extract resources from air.

## Power Use and Overclocking

The Particle Stabilizer consumes 32 kW while cooling down, and also consumes 32 kW after cooling is complete.

The Particle Stabilizer supports [overclocking](301_overclocking).

Without overclocking, the Particle Stabilizer takes 30 seconds (6000 ticks) to finish cooling. It can only be overclocked while cooling down. Each overclocking level consumes an additional 32 kW and doubles the cooling speed.

## Resource Extraction

When an <ref item="nekoplus:air_filter"/> is placed in the Particle Stabilizer input slot, it can extract resources from air. Different dimensions provide different air resources.
