# High Energy Laser

<item id="nekoplus:high_energy_laser"/>

The High Energy Laser is stronger than the <ref item="anvilcraft:ruby_laser"/> in almost every way. It emits a purple high-energy laser beam.

- 64x power use = 1024 kW
- 64x laser level
- 64x damage cap
  - This means the high-energy laser beam has a damage cap of 1024

## High-Energy Laser Beam

A high-energy laser beam requires every laser source in the current laser network to be a High Energy Laser. Adding any <ref item="anvilcraft:ruby_laser"/> to ~~try to cheat the system~~ will turn the beam back into a normal red laser.

High-energy laser beams can be used to build simpler [laser etching recipe](302_laser_etching_recipe) automation.

High-energy laser beams can be refracted by a <ref item="anvilcraft:ruby_prism"/> without turning back into normal red lasers.
