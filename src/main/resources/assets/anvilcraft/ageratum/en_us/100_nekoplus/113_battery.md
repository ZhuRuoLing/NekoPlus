# Battery and Huge Battery

<item id="nekoplus:battery"/>

When the power grid has spare power, the Battery charges. Charging does not occupy grid power; the amount of spare power determines the charging rate.

When the power grid is overloaded, the Battery discharges. While discharging, the Battery automatically allocates power according to the grid's missing power.

Charging and discharging are lossless. The charging and discharging rate limits can be adjusted in the GUI with the mouse wheel.
